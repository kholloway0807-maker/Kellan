# NOVA — Instagram Reel Strategy

> Ready-to-execute Reel concept for **NOVA** (novaly.us) — a motivational
> streetwear brand where every piece carries a mantra. Garment-dyed, broken-in,
> built for people chasing something. **Hero product: the $28 Leap Of Faith Tee**
> — the low-friction entry that turns cold scrollers into first-time buyers, with
> the hoodie and crewneck as the upsell.

## 1. HOOK (first 1–3s) — 3 variations

No logo in the first 2 seconds. The fabric and the price gap do the talking.

**Hook A — Bold claim (value shock)**
- *Visual:* Tight macro of the heavyweight fabric being stretched and snapped back — you can *see* the weight.
- *Text overlay:* "The $28 tee that feels like $80."

**Hook B — Pain point (relatable)**
- *Visual:* Person holds up a thin, see-through gray tee to the light, then drops it.
- *Text overlay:* "If I can see through your tee, it cost you $5 to look cheap."

**Hook C — Pattern interrupt (surprising fact)**
- *Visual:* Extreme close-up, water pouring over the tee; garment-dyed color blooms richer instead of fading.
- *Text overlay:* "Why this $28 tee looks 2 years old on day one 👇"

- **Lighting:** Golden hour for aspirational beats; soft window light for UGC. One warm practical for the fabric macro so the dye glows and the weight reads.
- **Color & mood:** Washed neutrals — black, true navy, bone — with sunset-orange spill. Warm, grounded, premium-thrift. Not flashy; *earned.*
- **Props/set:** Worn-in apartment or brick exterior, a mirror, the thin "before" tee, a glass of water for the dye demo. Nothing staged-looking.
- **Talent:** One person, 20–28, quietly ambitious — gym build or creative type. Energy: grounded confidence, not hype. The mantra carries the emotion.

## 4. AUDIO STRATEGY

- **Layering:** Use **both** — a trending sound at low bed + a short VO. Also ship a **text-only cut** (mantras + price on screen) for muted autoplay scrollers.
- **3 trending audio directions:**
  1. **Emotional / motivational build** — slow piano-to-drums crescendo that peaks on the walk-away. Best for the aspirational angle.
  2. **Hard / confident hip-hop or trap** — current sound with a heavy drop synced to pulling the tee on (0:09). Best for streetwear energy + shares.
  3. **Satisfying / ASMR-leaning** — quiet fabric and water sounds under a soft beat for the garment-dyed close-up. Best for saves.
- **SFX retention moments:**
  - Fabric snap synced to the stretch macro (0:00).
  - Beat drop on the tee going on (0:09).
  - Satisfying water/fabric ASMR on the dye bloom (0:12–0:16).
  - Footstep + ambient swell on the walk-away (0:19).

## 7. POSTING STRATEGY

- **Best time:** **Sunday 6:00–8:00pm** or **Thursday 7:00–9:00pm CDT** — when 18–30s plan the week / scroll aspirational content. Backup: Tue 12:30pm.
- **First-30-min engagement bait:**
  - Pin a comment: *"Drop your 2026 goal below — I'm replying to every single one with a code 👇"* (drives high-volume comments + DMs).
  - Reply within 30 min to every comment; auto-DM a first-order code to anyone who comments their goal.
- **Story funnel (3 frames):**
  1. **Tease:** Reel clip + "the drop is live 🔥" + poll: "Could you see through your current tee? 😅"
  2. **Proof:** The garment-dye water close-up + "this is why $28 here beats $15 anywhere else" + "Watch the Reel" sticker.
  3. **Convert:** Walk-away shot + "Start at $28" + Link sticker → novaly.us, with a first-order code sticker.
- **Paid boost (if budget):** $50–100 over 4 days. Objective: **Link clicks / Sales**. Audience: lookalike of site visitors + interests (streetwear brands, gym, faith/motivation, Represent/Gymshark/Vuori), US, 18–30. Boost only after it clears ~55% 3s-retention organically.

## 8. CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

- **Landing page (match the Reel):** Link straight to novaly.us with the **Leap Of Faith Tee** as the hero image + mantra above the fold — visual continuity from Reel to store = trust. First line of copy should echo the hook: *"Heavyweight. Garment-dyed. Feels like $80, costs $28."*
- **Offer / incentive + AOV play:** The $28 tee is the front door — keep the *first* purchase friction-low. Then upsell on the product page and at cart: **"Add the Take The Risk Hoodie and complete the set."** A first-order code (e.g. **RISK10**) plus a "spend $60, free shipping" threshold nudges the tee buyer toward tee + hoodie ($72) and lifts AOV well past the $28 entry.
- **Viewers → followers AND buyers in one Reel:** The mantra + aspirational walk-away earns the **follow**. The "$28, feels like $80" value math + "comment your goal for a code" earns the **click/DM**. The low price point closes the **first sale** with minimal hesitation; the cart upsell grows the order. Three capture points, one asset.

---

## 9. PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS TO TARGET

- **3-second retention:** **60%+** (the fabric stretch macro + "$28 feels like $80" claim).
- **Full watch / avg watch:** **40%+** (keep ≤22s; the dye-bloom at 12s is the mid-retention save).
- **Save rate:** **5%+** — the "looks 2 yrs old day one" fact is the save trigger.
- **Share trigger:** The **see-through-tee callout + the $28-feels-like-$80 value gap**. People send it with *"see, this is what I mean about cheap tees"* — it's a flex *and* an argument-settler.

---

## 10. A/B VARIATIONS TO TEST

- **Version A — Aesthetic / aspirational:** Golden-hour, cinematic, VO-led, walk-away hero in the tee. Sells the *identity*. Best for cold reach + follows.
- **Version B — Raw UGC / relatable:** Phone-shot "honest tee review" — "I stopped buying $15 tees, here's why," holds NOVA next to a thin one. Best for trust + DMs.
- **Version C — Educational / surprising fact:** Lead with "Why a $28 heavyweight garment-dyed tee beats a $15 printed one," show the water/fade + see-through demos side by side. Best for saves + shares (the secret angle).

Ship A and C first. Add B once you have someone comfortable on camera.
