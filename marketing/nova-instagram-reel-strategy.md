# NOVA — Instagram Reel Strategy

> Ready-to-execute Reel concept for **NOVA** (novaly.us) — a motivational
> streetwear brand where every piece carries a mantra. Garment-dyed, broken-in,
> built for people chasing something. Designed to maximize views, saves, profile
> visits, and click-throughs to the store.

## 1. HOOK (first 1–3s) — 3 variations

No logo in the first 2 seconds. The garment and the mantra do the talking.

**Hook A — Bold claim (aspirational)**
- *Visual:* Tight, slow-mo pull of the hoodie drawstring; "TAKE THE RISK" print catches the light.
- *Text overlay:* "The hoodie you buy right before your life changes."

**Hook B — Pain point (relatable)**
- *Visual:* Person dumps a pile of plain gray tees on the bed, unimpressed — then pulls the NOVA tee out of frame.
- *Text overlay:* "Your closet says nothing about you. On purpose?"

**Hook C — Pattern interrupt (surprising fact)**
- *Visual:* Extreme close-up of fabric, water pouring over it; the garment-dyed color blooms richer instead of fading.
- *Text overlay:* "Why this $44 hoodie looks 2 years old on day one 👇"

## 3. VISUAL DIRECTION — shot-by-shot

| # | Shot | Movement | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Drawstring / "TAKE THE RISK" macro | Slow push-in, slow-mo | The scroll-stopper; shoot at 120fps |
| 2 | Pile of boring gray basics | Handheld, slightly messy | Sells the pain, keep it raw |
| 3 | Reaching past cheap tee → NOVA | Quick hand POV | Decision beat |
| 4 | Pulling the hoodie on, mirror check | Handheld eye-level | Identity moment — confident, calm |
| 5 | Fabric + water, color blooms | Locked macro | THE save-bait shot, hold 2s |
| 6 | Three-piece mantra montage | Match-cut on movement | Hoodie → crewneck → tee |
| 7 | Walk-away in golden light | Slow trailing follow | Aspirational exhale; logo lands here |
| 8 | (opt.) Talking-head reaction | Handheld, eye-level | UGC trust beat for Version B |

- **Lighting:** Golden hour for aspirational beats; soft window light for UGC. One warm practical for the fabric macro so the dye glows.
- **Color & mood:** Washed neutrals — black, true navy, bone — with sunset-orange spill. Warm, grounded, premium-thrift. Not flashy; *earned.*
- **Props/set:** Worn-in apartment or brick exterior, a mirror, the pile of "before" basics, a glass of water for the dye demo. Nothing staged-looking.
- **Talent:** One person, 20–28, quietly ambitious — gym build or creative type. Energy: grounded confidence, not hype. The mantra carries the emotion.

## 4. AUDIO STRATEGY

- **Layering:** Use **both** — a trending sound at low bed + a short VO. Also ship a **text-only cut** (mantras on screen) for muted autoplay scrollers.
- **3 trending audio directions:**
  1. **Emotional / motivational build** — slow piano-to-drums crescendo that peaks on the walk-away. Best for the aspirational angle.
  2. **Hard / confident hip-hop or trap** — current sound with a heavy drop synced to pulling the hoodie on (0:09). Best for streetwear energy + shares.
  3. **Satisfying / ASMR-leaning** — quiet fabric and water sounds under a soft beat for the garment-dyed close-up. Best for saves.
- **SFX retention moments:**
  - Beat drop synced to the hoodie going on (0:09).
  - Satisfying water/fabric ASMR on the dye bloom (0:12–0:16).
  - Clean whoosh on each mantra match-cut (0:16).
  - Footstep + ambient swell on the walk-away (0:19).

## 7. POSTING STRATEGY

- **Best time:** **Sunday 6:00–8:00pm** or **Thursday 7:00–9:00pm CDT** — when 18–30s plan the week / scroll aspirational content. Backup: Tue 12:30pm.
- **First-30-min engagement bait:**
  - Pin a comment: *"Drop your 2026 goal below — I'm replying to every single one with a code 👇"* (drives high-volume comments + DMs).
  - Reply within 30 min to every comment; auto-DM a first-order code to anyone who comments their goal.
- **Story funnel (3 frames):**
  1. **Tease:** Reel clip + "the drop is live 🔥" + poll: "Hoodie or crewneck?"
  2. **Proof:** The garment-dye water close-up + "this is why it doesn't fade like the cheap ones" + "Watch the Reel" sticker.
  3. **Convert:** Walk-away shot + "Shop the mantras" + Link sticker → novaly.us. Add a "first order 10% off" code sticker.
- **Paid boost (if budget):** $50–100 over 4 days. Objective: **Link clicks / Sales**. Audience: lookalike of site visitors + interests (streetwear brands, gym, faith/motivation, Represent/Gymshark/Vuori), US, 18–30. Boost only after it clears ~55% 3s-retention organically.

## 8. CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

- **Landing page (match the Reel):** Link straight to novaly.us with the hero image being the same hoodie + "Take the Risk" mantra above the fold — visual continuity from Reel to store = trust. First line of copy should echo the hook: *"Garment-dyed. Broken in from day one."* Show the three mantras as a collection so the Reel's montage maps 1:1 to what they land on.
- **Offer / incentive:** First-order code (e.g. **RISK10**) surfaced in the pinned comment, Story, and bio. Add a "complete the set" nudge (hoodie + tee bundle) to lift AOV above the $44 entry.
- **Viewers → followers AND buyers in one Reel:** The mantra + aspirational walk-away earns the **follow** (identity content people want in their feed). The "comment your goal for a code" earns the **DM/click**. The Story link + code closes the **sale** same-session. Three capture points, one asset.

---

## 9. PERFORMANCE BENCHMARKS TO TARGET

- **3-second retention:** **60%+** (the drawstring macro + bold claim).
- **Full watch / avg watch:** **40%+** (keep ≤22s; the dye-bloom at 12s is the mid-retention save).
- **Save rate:** **5%+** — the "looks 2 yrs old day one" fact is the save trigger.
- **Share trigger:** The **garment-dyed water demo + the mantras**. People send it with *"this is so me / we need these"* — the mantra makes it a personality flex, not just a product.

---

## 10. A/B VARIATIONS TO TEST

- **Version A — Aesthetic / aspirational:** Golden-hour, cinematic, VO-led, walk-away hero. Sells the *identity*. Best for cold reach + follows.
- **Version B — Raw UGC / relatable:** Phone-shot "honest closet review" — "I stopped buying plain basics, here's why," tries on all three pieces. Best for trust + DMs.
- **Version C — Educational / surprising fact:** Lead with "Why garment-dyed beats printed every time," show the water/fade demo vs a cracking cheap print. Best for saves + shares (the secret angle).

Ship A and C first. Add B once you have someone comfortable on camera.
